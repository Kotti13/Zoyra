ABOUT ME

I like building the whole thing.

I am a software engineer building business applications, ERP solutions, HRMS systems and user-focused digital products. I work in Python and JavaScript, with React and Next.js on the front end and FastAPI and the Frappe Framework on the back end.

Zoyra started as a question: what would a music app feel like if it were built around the people you listen with? It grew into a full product, with encrypted chat, calls, listening rooms and Forever friends, all designed and built by me. I am also exploring AI, LLMs and automation.

Based in Chennai, India. Mechanical engineering diploma, then nine months at FSSA Software Academy. Learn. Build. Improve. Repeat.
