PRIVACY

Your music is social. Your messages are yours.

A plain‑language summary of what Zoyra stores, what stays on your device, and the controls you have. The code is public, so you can check every claim on GitHub.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Encrypted chats

1:1 messages are end‑to‑end encrypted. Chat search decrypts on your device, not on our servers.

Export or delete

Download everything stored about you, or delete your account, from Settings › Privacy.

You choose who reaches you

Control DMs, last seen and online status. Restrict or block anyone.

No card details here

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. Zoyra never sees your card.

What we store

  • Your account: email or phone number, username, profile photo, banner and bio.
  • Your library: likes, playlists, follows, downloads list and listening history (used for Home, Daily Mix, Wrapped, Blend and taste match).
  • Social content you post: stories, notes, comments and community posts.
  • Encrypted messages, which we cannot read.
  • Your plan status, set by the store's purchase confirmation.

What stays on your device

  • Your chat encryption keys. You can back them up with a PIN you choose.
  • Downloaded songs and local music from your phone.
  • Biometric data: Face ID and fingerprint checks are done by your phone, never sent to us.

Permissions, and why we ask

  • Microphone: voice search, voice notes, karaoke and calls.
  • Camera and photos: profile pictures, playlist covers, chat photos and mood scan.
  • Location: run distance and route in run mode. Only while you use it.
  • Notifications: messages, calls and releases, with quiet hours you control.

Every permission is optional. Zoyra works without the ones you decline.

Your controls

  • Two‑factor authentication and app or per‑chat biometric lock.
  • See active sessions and sign out other devices.
  • Disappearing messages: 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days.
  • Hide explicit content, or specific songs and artists.
  • Kids mode for younger listeners on a Family plan.

Services we work with

Zoyra uses trusted providers to run the app: Supabase for accounts and data, Agora for calls, RevenueCat with Apple and Google for purchases, Sentry for crash reports, and Expo for push notifications. Each receives only what it needs for its job.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue on GitHub. Code is open for review on GitHub.
