THE NAME, DECODED

ZOYA + AURA = ZOYRA
ZOYA Means "alive", "full of life". The energy you feel when the right song comes on.
AURA The glow around a person: the mood and feeling they carry into a room.
ZOYRA A living aura. Music that makes you feel alive, and the glow it leaves between you and your people.

WHAT'S INSIDE

One app for listening, talking and staying close.

A player that feels alive

Gapless and crossfade, a 10‑band equalizer, sleep timer, car mode, lock‑screen controls and a queue you can reorder by hand.

Synced lyrics and karaoke

Time‑synced lyrics in Fill, Wave or Word styles, a focus mode, karaoke with word fill, and shareable lyric cards.

AI DJ and AI playlists

Describe a vibe, even "no heavy drums", and get a playlist. Or let the AI DJ mix sources for you. Mood scan reads a photo.

Chat Pro, end‑to‑end encrypted

Voice notes, view‑once photos, songs and lyric lines in chat, scheduled messages, time capsules and per‑chat Face ID lock.

Calls and listening rooms

Voice and video calls, group calls, and rooms where everyone hears the same song at the same moment.

Forever friends

An opt‑in, permanent bond with your closest person: a gold ring, a shared streak and a private vault. See how it works

Run mode and wellness

Pick a tempo and Zoyra matches music to your pace, with GPS distance, cadence and a shareable run summary.

Family and kids mode

Up to 6 accounts on one Family plan. Kids mode hides explicit tracks and restricts messaging.
