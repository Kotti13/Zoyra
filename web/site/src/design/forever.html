WHY WE BUILT IT

Apps make it easy to leave. We wanted one that helps you stay.

Most social apps are built around the exit: mute, unfollow, block, gone. Those tools matter, and Zoyra has all of them. But the people who've been with you through the long nights and the loud mornings deserve something the other way round.

Forever is a choice two people make on purpose. It doesn't rank friends or count followers. It just marks one bond as permanent, and gives it a little ritual: a song a day, a ring that stays gold, a vault that only opens for two.

Questions about Forever

Who can I make my forever friend?

Anyone you can message on Zoyra. They receive a request and decide; if they don't accept, nothing changes between you.

What does "can't be blocked" mean?

Once you're both forever, the bond is permanent and blocking is turned off between the two of you. That's why it only begins when both people agree.

How does the streak work?

Share music with each other to keep the streak going. Your current count shows on the gold ring.

What goes in the vault?

The songs, notes and moments you save together. It's visible only to the two of you.

Is Forever available in Kids mode?

Kids mode restricts messaging, so social bonds like Forever follow those limits.

Who's the first person you'd send a song to?

Download Zoyra and send them a forever request.
